Caleres Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results
Third Quarter & Full Year Outlook
For full-year 2026, we anticipate total sales to increase low-to-mid-single digits. Brand Portfolio sales are expected to be up low-double digits. Famous Footwear sales and comparable sales are expected to be down low-to-mid-single digits. Consolidated gross margin is expected to improve 180 to 220 basis points.
