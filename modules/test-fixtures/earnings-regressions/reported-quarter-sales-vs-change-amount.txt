Lockheed Martin Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Sales increased 11% to $20.7 billion.
BETHESDA, Md., Oct. 22, 2026 – Lockheed Martin Corporation today reported third quarter 2026 sales of $20.7 billion, compared to $18.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025.
Sales: Third quarter 2026 sales increased $2.1 billion, or 11%, driven by growth in each of the company's four business segments.
