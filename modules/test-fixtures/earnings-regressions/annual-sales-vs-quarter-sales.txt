MasterCraft Boat Holdings, Inc. Reports Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results
Fourth Quarter Results
For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, MasterCraft Boat Holdings, Inc. reported consolidated net sales of $129.9 million, up $50.4 million from the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. The increase in net sales was primarily due to the acquisition.
Fiscal 2026 Results
For fiscal 2026, MasterCraft Boat Holdings, Inc. reported consolidated net sales of $348.9 million, up $64.7 million from fiscal 2025.
