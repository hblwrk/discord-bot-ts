NextEra Energy reports third-quarter 2026 financial results
JUNO BEACH, Fla. - NextEra Energy, Inc. (NYSE: NEE) today reported 2026 third-quarter net income attributable to NextEra Energy on a GAAP basis of $4.775 billion, or $2.28 per share, compared to $2.438 billion, or $1.18 per share, for the third quarter of 2025. On an adjusted basis, NextEra Energy's 2026 third-quarter earnings were $2.592 billion, or $1.24 per share, compared to $2.348 billion, or $1.13 per share, in the third quarter of 2025.

FPL reported third-quarter 2026 net income of $1.62 billion, or $0.77 per share, compared to $1.463 billion, or $0.71 per share, for the prior-year comparable quarter.
Corporate and Other
In the third quarter of 2026 on a GAAP basis, Corporate and Other results increased $0.95 per share, compared to the prior-year quarter. On an adjusted basis, Corporate and Other results for the third quarter of 2026 decreased $0.01 per share, compared to the prior-year quarter.
